<h1 align="center">Hull</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Everything your Tank 4 can do, in one place.</b></p>
<p align="center"><sub>by Herculade0</sub></p>

<br>

## What's inside

### 🔦 Torch
- Control the **red** and **blue** signal lights individually
- Turn on the camp light freely, without the lockouts in the stock Unihertz app
- Quick settings tiles for one-swipe control

### 💿 TankBoot
- Present the phone as a **Ventoy multi-ISO** boot drive or a single-ISO USB CD-ROM
- Keep a library of ISOs on the phone and pick one at boot from a themed menu

### ⌨️ Injecta
- Acts as a **USB HID keyboard** on any computer, with no drivers needed
- Saved macros and one-shot typing for long commands and setup scripts
- Can type a password pulled from your password manager at run time, with nothing secret stored

## On the way

### 🔌 TankLink
- **Bench gateway** over a USB-C Ethernet adapter, with NAT to the internet or a fully air-gapped mode
- **PXE / ProxyDHCP** server with bundled iPXE, so you can network-boot ISOs from the phone
- **Switch discovery** (LLDP/CDP) shows the switch, port and VLAN you're plugged into
- Packet capture to `.pcap`, VLAN tester and Wake-on-LAN
- Only ever touches the USB Ethernet port, never Wi-Fi or cellular

### 🖥️ TankKVM
- Plug in a cheap UVC capture card and see a headless machine's screen on the phone
- Type and click back into it through Injecta, like a pocket crash cart

### 💾 Absorb USB
- Plug in a USB stick and pull its ISOs straight into TankBoot's library

### 🧵 TankTerm
- Serial console for routers, switches and dev boards over USB serial adapters

## Getting it

> ⚠️ **Root required.** Hull needs a rooted Tank 4 (Magisk). Rooting is on you; we don't provide guides or support for it.

Hull is in private beta and shared with testers directly. There are no downloads here.
